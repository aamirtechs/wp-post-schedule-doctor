WP Post Schedule Doctor

A zero-dependency Python CLI that audits a WordPress posts CSV export for missed schedules and scheduling mistakes: posts whose publish time has passed without publishing, posts missed by more than a week, posts scheduled in the far future (usually a typo year), drafts sitting untouched for months, two posts sharing one publish slot, posts with no title or no author, duplicate post ids, malformed rows, and invalid or future dates. It also prints a per-author summary table, so a site owner can see whose queue is slipping.
